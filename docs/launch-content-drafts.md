# Launch content drafts (LinkedIn / TikTok / forums)

Rule for all of it: **give away genuinely useful data in every post.** The product sells because
the free samples are good, not because the posts ask for the sale.

## LinkedIn post 1 — the origin story (launch day)
> Applying for finance placements, I got tired of fighting 3,000 applicants for the same 50
> firms on every job board. So I built a scraper on the Companies House and FCA registers.
> It found **1,100+ boutique UK finance firms** — M&A shops, wealth managers, prop firms —
> that almost never advertise roles. I sent cold emails to firms nobody else was writing to.
> [1–2 sentence honest outcome.] I've now made the whole database **free for any student** →
> [link]. No catch — I built it for myself and it costs nothing to share.

## Forum answers (r/FinancialCareers, StudentRoom, society Discords)
Answer "how do I get a placement with no grad scheme?" questions genuinely, mention the free
5-firm sample only where rules allow self-promo. Never spam.

## University finance societies
DM the committee: offer a **free society-wide sample pack** (20 firms for their city) in
exchange for a mention in their newsletter. Societies need content; this is content.
