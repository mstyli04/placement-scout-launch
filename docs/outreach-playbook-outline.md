# The Boutique Outreach Playbook — outline (Styli writes this; ~1 evening)

Included with every purchase. Target: 8–12 pages / one long Notion page.

1. **Why boutiques** — maths of 3,000 applicants per bank role vs 0–5 emails a boutique receives;
   what boutique experience is actually like (real responsibility, deal exposure).
2. **How to read a firm** — decoding the database columns: what the score means, why
   FCA-authorised matters, what a careers page's existence (or absence) tells you.
3. **Picking your 30** — filter to city + sector fit; the 10/10/10 rule (10 reach, 10 realistic,
   10 recently-authorised); why "new firms" are the highest-reply segment.
4. **Finding the right person** — firm website → FCA register individuals → LinkedIn triangulation;
   always a named partner/founder at small firms, never info@ first.
5. **The email** — 4-line structure (specific hook about *their* deals / why *them* / what you
   bring in one line / small ask: 15-min call). Annotated real examples, good and bad.
6. **The follow-up cadence** — day 4 bump, day 10 new-angle, then stop; tracking in the sheet's
   Status/Who/Notes columns.
7. **Converting a call into a placement** — questions to ask, how to propose an informal
   internship when none is advertised.

Personal experience from Styli's own outreach makes sections 5–7 authentic — that authenticity
is the moat over generic career-advice content.
